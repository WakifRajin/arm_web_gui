Both motors must have torque ON. Pitch/roll are clamped to the limits below before mixing. If a legal pitch+roll combination would still ask a motor to travel past its own limit, both targets are scaled back together (direction preserved, magnitude reduced) rather than letting one motor clamp on its own — the Event Log shows the achieved pitch/roll when that happens.

Mixing Config (verify on the bench before trusting this)

Default convention: motor A = pitch + roll, motor B = pitch − roll. With torque OFF or a low speed/current limit, jog pitch a small amount alone (roll = 0) and confirm the wrist tips the way you expect; do the same for roll alone. If pitch and roll are swapped rather than reversed (pitch input moves it like roll), use "Swap pitch/roll axes" instead of a sign flip — a sign only reverses a direction, it can't exchange the two axes.

Failsafe controls

Bring-up reopens the OS-level SocketCAN link (and reconnects the backend to it). Bring-down is a hard kill one level below E-STOP — it E-STOPs first, then cuts CAN traffic at the OS level entirely, useful if a motor won't stop responding to disable frames.
