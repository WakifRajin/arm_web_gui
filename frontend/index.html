Mixing Config (verify on the bench before trusting this)

Default convention: motor A = pitch + roll, motor B = pitch − roll. With torque OFF or a low speed/current limit, jog pitch alone (roll = 0) and confirm the wrist tips the way you expect, then do the same for roll alone. If pitch and roll are swapped rather than reversed, use "Swap pitch/roll axes" instead of a sign flip.
